Bench wiring

The ESP32-S3-Zero and the 15 buttons on an MB-102 breadboard, no soldering, then the jumpers from the Pi's GPIO header to the WM8960 audio board and the 4-inch screen. Same pin plan as the D2K_V1 schematic in Fusion. Hole names are column 1–63 and row a–j.

Breadboard: controls

D-padA B X YSelect Start HomeL1 R1 L2 R2GND
  • Reading a hole name: a22 is column 22, row a. The five holes of one column in the same half (a–e, or f–j) are joined inside the board, so a wire can go in any free hole of that group.
  • The ESP32-S3-Zero sits across the centre channel: its TX/RX/13…7 pin row in row d and its 5V/GND/3V3/1…6 row in row h, columns 2–10, USB-C off the left edge. Every wire starts in a free hole next to a pin (row a above, row j below) and ends in a free hole of a switch column.
  • Each 6×6 switch straddles the channel with its legs in rows e and f of two columns. One column takes the GPIO wire, the other a short black wire to the GND rail. The ESP32's internal pull-ups do the rest: no resistors. If a button reads as always pressed, turn the switch 90°.
  • No two button wires cross. The dotted link on the GND rail is only needed if your board's rail is split in the middle. Hover or long-press a wire to see its name.

Before you power up

  • Do not hold SELECT while the ESP32 powers up. It is wired to the TX pin, which the chip drives during boot. (The firmware turns TX into a normal input afterwards.)
  • Circle Pads later: measure their pinout first (VCC, GND, X, Y are not verified) and feed them 3.3 V, never 5 V. They will take GPIO1–4, so L1 R1 L2 R2 move then.
  • Nothing on the ESP32 sees 5 V. Its pins are 3.3 V only; leave its 5V pin empty, the USB-C powers it.
  • The 4-inch lead is red on pin 4, black on pin 6. The Pi's 5 V pins are not fused.
  • WM8960 header pins 5 and 7 are SCL and SDA per its schematic: check the silkscreen. Its speaker amplifier runs from the Pi's 3.3 V pin, so keep the volume moderate until its current is measured.
  • Speakers: each one between its P and N pins only (bridged outputs), never to GND. Their PH1.25 plugs need a 4-pin adapter or a recrimp for the board's SPK connector.
  • Power off the Pi before plugging the DSI ribbon or the GPIO jumpers.
